# Microsoft Corporation (MSFT) — Equity Research & Financial Statement Analysis

**Finance | Equity Research | Financial Modeling | Investment Analysis**

An academic equity research project examining the financial performance, profitability, liquidity, and financial position of **Microsoft Corporation (NASDAQ: MSFT)**.

This project was developed as part of a finance and financial analysis assignment at **Baruch College — Zicklin School of Business**.

---

## Project Overview

The objective of this project is to evaluate Microsoft's financial performance using financial statement analysis, financial ratios, and historical trends.

The research explores the company's operating performance, financial stability, capital structure, and investment-related risks to develop an informed perspective on its financial fundamentals.

## Research Objectives

- Analyze financial performance through historical financial statements.
- Examine revenue growth and profitability trends.
- Evaluate liquidity and financial stability.
- Assess leverage and capital structure.
- Identify potential financial strengths, weaknesses, and investment risks.
- Connect financial analysis with investment decision-making.

## Financial Analysis Framework

| Analysis Area | Focus |
|---|---|
| Revenue Growth | Historical sales performance and growth trends |
| Profitability | Operating margin, net margin, and earnings performance |
| Liquidity | Current ratio and short-term financial obligations |
| Leverage | Capital structure, debt exposure, and solvency |
| Financial Trends | Changes in major financial indicators over time |
| Investment Analysis | Financial fundamentals, risks, and investment considerations |

## Research Methodology

The project follows a structured financial analysis approach:

1. **Financial Statement Review:** Examine reported financial information and financial position.
2. **Ratio Analysis:** Evaluate profitability, liquidity, and leverage indicators.
3. **Trend Analysis:** Identify patterns and changes in financial performance.
4. **Risk Assessment:** Consider financial and business risks affecting Microsoft.
5. **Investment Perspective:** Interpret the financial findings in the context of equity research.
